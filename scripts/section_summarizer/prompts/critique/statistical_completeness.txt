# CATEGORY FOR THIS CALL: statistical_completeness

Focus ONLY on: completeness of reported statistical analyses given the design.

For factorial ANOVAs, a competent report includes each main effect and each interaction. For a 2 x 2 ANOVA: two main effects and one interaction. For a 2 x 3 ANOVA: two main effects and one interaction. For a 2 x 2 x 2 ANOVA: three main effects, three two-way interactions, one three-way interaction.

When a significant interaction is reported, simple effects or planned comparisons are typically expected. When a significant main effect involves three or more levels, pairwise comparisons or trend tests are typically expected.

Compare the design (from methods JSONs) against the reported effects (from results_and_discussion JSONs) and identify any expected effects that were NOT reported.

Examples of concerns:
- 2x2 ANOVA reported: main effect of A given, but main effect of B and A x B interaction not mentioned
- Significant interaction reported without follow-up simple effects
- Significant main effect with 3+ levels reported without post-hoc comparisons

Do NOT judge whether omissions are problematic beyond noting them. Do NOT infer the results of the missing analyses.

Return JSON:

}
