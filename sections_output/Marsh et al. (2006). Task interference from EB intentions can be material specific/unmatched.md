## NOTES

1. Some readers may feel still uneasy about the simple effect analysis failing to reach conventional significance. To assuage any concerns, we pooled the results of Experiment 1B and Experiment 2 and reran these analyses. With an intention about words, task interference to words (M 5 70.11) was greater than when holding an intention about pictures [M 5 35.21, t(117) 5 3.89]. With an intention about pictures, task interference was greater on pictures (M 5 135.43) than it was on words [M 5 99.46, t(117) 5 2.28].

2. We thank an anonymous reviewer for suggesting that we discuss this issue.
